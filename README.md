# Interactive-Excel-Dashboard-for-Beverage-Retailers-in-US
This Dashboard was part of my Assignment for Data Analytics Foundation Class.
The dashboard includes data on six beverage brands sold by six retailers across the Northeast, South, Southeast, Midwest and West for the last 2 years 2022 and 2023; total sales of $12,016,665.
Sales Growth: The market grew at a strong pace, with revenue doubling from $2.4M in 2022 to $9.6M in 2023, showing strong market growth on all brands and regions.
Made up of Coca-Cola's bestselling $2.77M in beverage sales, Dasani Water's $2.39M, and Diet Coke's $2.06M, the top three brands were Coca-Cola, Dasani Water and Diet Coke. Fanta had the worst performance at $1.43M.
The top Retailer was West Soda, who reported a total of $3.24M in sales, closely followed by BevCo at $2.79M and FizzyCo at $2.42M. Amazon and Walmart were not among them.
Quarterly Trend: Sales and operating margins were very consistent throughout 2023 and were at a peak of $2.8M in Q3 2023, with margins around 42-43% throughout.
Profitability: Total operating profit was $4.72M, with an average operating margin of about .42% for all transactions.
